# UI/UX Design Brief

**Project Name:** DuskyMoon Productions Official Website  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Design Philosophy:** "Cinematic Minimalism" – Premium, dark, and immersive. The music and the artists must take center stage, unobstructed by visual clutter. 

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Aesthetic
The website must feel like a high-end, exclusive record label. It should evoke the feeling of stepping into a modern, dimly lit recording studio—sophisticated, serious, and focused on art.

*   **Vibe:** Dark, sleek, mysterious, yet highly professional and trustworthy.
*   **Keywords:** Premium, Cinematic, Minimalist, Forward-Thinking, Immersive.
*   **Inspiration References:** OVO Sound, TDE (Top Dawg Entertainment), or modern high-end fashion e-commerce (like Noir Studio).

## 2. Color Palette
The color scheme must be heavily weighted towards deep blacks and charcoals to allow album artwork and artist photography to pop.

*   **Primary Background:** `Deep Void` (#0A0A0A) – Not pure black, but a rich, deep charcoal for depth.
*   **Surface / Card Backgrounds:** `Obsidian` (#121212) or (#171717) with subtle borders (`#2A2A2A`).
*   **Primary Text:** `Off-White` (#EAEAEA) – Softer on the eyes than pure white, ensuring readability on dark backgrounds.
*   **Secondary Text (Subtitles/Muted):** `Ash Grey` (#A3A3A3).
*   **Accent/Action Color:** A very subtle, muted metallic tone—perhaps a `Brushed Steel` (#8E8E8E) or a pale, desaturated blue/purple hinting at the "DuskyMoon" theme, used *only* for hover states or active links.

## 3. Typography
Typography must communicate authority and modernity.

*   **Headings (H1, H2, H3):** A bold, structured Sans-Serif.
    *   *Recommendation:* **Inter** (Bold/ExtraBold), **Clash Display**, or **Monument Extended**. This gives a brutalist, high-fashion edge to the label's name and headers.
*   **Body Text (Paragraphs, Bios, Links):** A clean, highly legible Sans-Serif.
    *   *Recommendation:* **Inter** (Regular/Medium) or **SF Pro Display**.
*   **Styling Rules:** Use generous tracking (letter-spacing) on main headers. Keep body text concise and well-leaded (line-height) to maintain the minimalist aesthetic.

## 4. UI Components & Interaction Design

### 4.1. The Hero Section (Home Page)
*   **Visual:** Full viewport height (`100vh`). A high-resolution, subtly animated background or a striking, moody still from the latest music video/artist shoot. 
*   **Overlay:** A subtle gradient overlay (black to transparent) to ensure text readability.
*   **Typography:** The latest release title dead center, massive and bold.
*   **CTA:** A minimalist, outline button (e.g., `[ STREAM 'SAPNE' ]`) that fills on hover.

### 4.2. Navigation Bar
*   **State:** Fixed/Sticky at the top, but initially transparent, blending into the hero section. As the user scrolls, it gains a frosted glass effect (backdrop-blur) with a semi-transparent `Obsidian` background.
*   **Links:** Clean text, no underlines. Hover effect should be a subtle change in opacity or a smooth color transition to the Accent Color.

### 4.3. Artist Roster & Release Grid
*   **Cards:** Borderless cards. The image is the primary element.
*   **Hover Effects (Micro-interactions):** This is where the premium feel lives.
    *   *On Image Hover:* A smooth, slow zoom-in effect (scale 1.05) on the image within its container, while the image darkens slightly.
    *   *On Hover (Data Reveal):* For releases, hovering over the album art could smoothly slide up the track name and a "Play" icon.
*   **Corners:** Very slight rounding (e.g., `rounded-sm` or `4px` radius) or sharp corners for a more brutalist, modern feel. Avoid heavy rounding.

### 4.4. The Footer
*   **Layout:** Symmetrical, clean, and spacious.
*   **The Anchor:** The "Founded by Kxrn" text must be elegant but prominent enough for bots to crawl. Use a muted color that brightens on hover.

## 5. User Experience (UX) Principles

*   **Zero Friction:** The path from landing on the site to streaming a track should be a maximum of two clicks.
*   **Image Optimization:** Because the design relies heavily on large, high-quality images (album art, artist portraits), aggressive lazy-loading and WebP formatting are non-negotiable to maintain the premium feel without sacrificing load speed.
*   **Mobile-First Supremacy:** 80%+ of music discovery happens on mobile. The mobile experience must not be an afterthought. Large tap targets, swipeable carousels for roster/releases, and a clean hamburger menu are essential.
*   **Absence of "Noise":** No pop-ups, no excessive animations, no auto-playing audio (unless specifically triggered by the user). Let the visual design breathe.
