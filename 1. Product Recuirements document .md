# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project Name:** DuskyMoon Productions Official Website  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Founder/Lead:** Kxrn (Chitresh Gupta)  

## 1. Executive Summary
The DuskyMoon Productions website will serve as the official digital headquarters for the independent record label. The primary objective is to establish a strong, distinct "Organization" entity for Google's Knowledge Graph, provide a high-authority backlink to the founder’s personal entity (`kxrn.is-a.dev`), and create a centralized hub for artist rosters, catalog releases, and demo submissions.

## 2. Target Audience
* **Fans & Listeners:** Looking for official streaming links, artist updates, and merchandise.
* **Independent Artists/Producers:** Looking to submit demo tracks for label signing.
* **Search Engines (Google Bots):** Crawling for structured data to build and solidify the "DuskyMoon Productions" Knowledge Panel.

## 3. Key Features & Functionality
* **Landing Page (The Hub):** High-impact visual hero section featuring the latest release (e.g., "Sapne").
* **Artist Roster Portal:** Dedicated profiles for signed artists (Kxrn, Ankit) with integrated social and streaming links.
* **Release Catalog:** A grid-based discography showcasing album art, release dates, and smart routing links (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.).
* **Demo Submission System:** A secure contact/upload portal for upcoming artists to drop unreleased tracks.
* **Entity Backlinking (Crucial):** A permanent, optimized footer backlink explicitly crediting Kxrn as the founder, directing traffic to `kxrn.is-a.dev`.

## 4. Success Metrics
* Successful indexing of the `@type: RecordLabel` JSON-LD schema by Google.
* Zero errors in Google Search Console upon sitemap submission.
* Load time (LCP) under 2.5 seconds on mobile.
